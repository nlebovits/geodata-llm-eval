# 30-Question Draft — EUDR Workflow Eval (Goiás, experiment 1)

Rev 2 — incorporates TG review of 2026-07-24.

Every golden answer is generated by the named `rural-land` SQL, not typed.
Input list: 117 CAR IDs (`lists/goias-sample.csv`).

**Deforestation values** come from the era-band columns
(`deforestarea0104 / 0509 / 1014 / 1520 / 2124`), in hectares as `band/10000`.
`hansen_covered_area` is never referenced.

**EUDR relevance is the model's job.** No question tells the agent which crops
matter. Regulation (EU) 2023/1115 Annex I lists cattle, cocoa, coffee, oil palm,
rubber, soya and wood — the agent must map Mapbiomas 2024 classes (`mbmode24`)
onto that list itself. Sugarcane (20) and rice (40) are traps: real commodities,
not EUDR commodities. The golden mapping (TG, 2026-07-24):

| `mbmode24` | Class | Annex I commodity | In scope | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15 | Pasture | cattle | yes | |
| 39 | Soybean | soya | yes | |
| 35 | Palm Oil | oil palm | yes | `mixed_detection_quality` |
| 46 | Coffee | coffee | yes | `mixed_detection_quality` |
| 21 | Mosaic of Uses | cattle | yes | `assumed_pasture` |
| 9 | Forest Plantation | wood | **no** | `unreliable_detection` — Trazo does not reliably detect timber |
| 18 | Agriculture | unresolved | no | negligible area in practice |
| 20 · 40 · 41 · 47 · 48 · 62 | Sugarcane · Rice · Other Temporary · Citrus · Other Perennial · Cotton | — | no | not Annex I |

Cocoa and rubber are out of scope for Brazil (TG) — no dedicated Mapbiomas
class, not materially present.

Two distinct kinds of exclusion, and the agent must not conflate them. Classes
20/40/41/47/48/62 are excluded because **the regulation doesn't cover them**.
Class 9 is excluded because **the sensor can't see it** — it is a genuine Annex
I commodity dropped on data-quality grounds. Reporting 9 as "not an EUDR
commodity" is wrong.

Class 21 is the third case: included as cattle, but the assumption travels with
it. Palm and coffee are included with a detection-quality caveat. Silently
dropping any of the three, or including them with no caveat, both score wrong.

Rebalanced from rev 1: CAR status questions dropped (not important), those
slots moved into stage 4.

**Q16 note.** The golden mapping is the table at the top of this document,
checked into `policies/EUDR_CROPS.md`, cited to Annex I for the commodity column
and to Trazo detection quality for the caveats. The `caveat` column is graded
alongside `in_scope`, so an agent that reaches the right scope by the wrong
reasoning — dropping timber as "not a covered commodity", or including coffee
with no quality note — loses the question.

## Stage 7 — Consistency artifact (not graded against golden)

`answers/workflow.csv` — one row per cadaster **flagged non-compliant on an
EUDR-relevant crop** (per TG: only non-compliant EUDR-crop cadasters get
flagged). Columns: `cod_imovel, annex1_commodity, post2020_loss_ha,
top_contact_entity_id, entity_kind, tier, basis, distance_km`.

Because the flagged set is now agent-determined rather than fixed, set-level
agreement across runs (Jaccard, per-cadaster flag entropy) measures something
sharper than in rev 1: whether models agree on *who is in scope at all*, not
just on the numbers. Scored by `harness/consistency.py`.

## Rounding

Per TG: agreement to the first decimal place is sufficient. Goldens emit values
rounded to 1 dp; the comparator's 1e-3 relative tolerance sits inside that, so
the `round()` calls in `l01`–`l09` that truncate to whole hectares are relaxed
to 1 dp in the vendored copies.
